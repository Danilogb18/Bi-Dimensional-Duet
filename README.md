## About

You are a little square living happily in a two-dimensional world. But one day,
your dad disappeared. He had been working on some strange experiments, and now
it's up to you to find him.

Your journey takes you through grassy hills, a scorching desert and dark
underground ruins, dodging spikes and saw blades, pushing crates, and facing
the strange geometric creatures waiting at the end of each area.

### Features

- Hand-drawn pixel art across multiple themed areas
- Platforming with hazards, crates and switches
- Boss fights with their own health bars
- Health system and dynamic lighting in underground levels

## Context

Developed in 2020 as a high school project. It was one of my first complete
games, built from scratch in Unity. The in-game text is in Spanish.

## Built with

- **Unity**
- **C#**

## Running the project

1. Clone the repository
2. Open the `Bi Dimensional Duet` folder with Unity Hub
3. Open the main scene in `Assets/Scenes/` and press Play

Or just [play it on itch.io](https://danilogb18.itch.io/bi-dimensional-duet).
